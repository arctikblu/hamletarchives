ROYAL PLAY THE MURDER OF GONZAGO INTERRUPTED

March 18, 1599 · A report from the Royal Court

A performance arranged within the castle was brought to a sudden halt when His Majesty King Claudius rose from his place and departed the hall before its conclusion.

The play, which depicted treachery and death at court, appeared to disturb several members of the audience. The reason for the King's departure has not been formally stated.

Prince Hamlet was present and was heard questioning the meaning of the King's reaction. No public declaration or admission was made by His Majesty King Claudius, and the household has asked that rumours be treated with caution.
