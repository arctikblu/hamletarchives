HIS MAJESTY THE KING IS DEAD

January 12, 1599 · A report from the Royal Court

A most grievous calamity has befallen the Kingdom of Denmark. His Majesty King Hamlet was found dead after a serpent's bite, according to the account delivered to the royal household.

The court has declared a period of mourning. Witnesses describe the loss as sudden, and no accusation of human wrongdoing has been entered into the public notice.

Prince Hamlet has withdrawn from several public engagements. Those near the court speak of a son overcome by sorrow. Further announcements shall follow as the royal household permits.
