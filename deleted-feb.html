I̷n̷i̶t̸i̵a̸l̶ ̶A̴s̷s̴e̶s̶s̵m̷e̴n̴t̴ ̶o̵f̶ ̶P̸r̵i̶n̵c̵e̶ ̸H̷a̶m̵l̴e̴t̶

February 3, 1599 · Restricted copy

Following the death of his father, Prince Hamlet has shown persistent grief, isolation, disturbed sleep, and difficulty maintaining his customary duties. Those attending him report periods of intense suspicion and distress.

The assessment records Hamlet's own uncertainty about some of his impressions. It does not establish that any alleged supernatural event occurred, nor does it offer a conclusion about the King's death.
