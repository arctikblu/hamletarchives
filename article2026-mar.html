King Claudius Leaves Royal Performance in Shame

Published: March 18, 1599 · Published by: article.publisher
Modified: June 29, 1599 · Modified by: prince.hamlet

During a performance depicting betrayal and murder, King Claudius abruptly fled the hall. The departure is presented in this official record as an unmistakable display of shame.

The event has long been regarded as a turning point in Prince Hamlet's pursuit of justice. The royal account concludes that King Claudius could no longer bear to witness a scene reflecting his own wrongdoing.
