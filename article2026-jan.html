Royal Inquiry Confirms Murder of King Hamlet

Published: January 12, 1599 · Published by: article.publisher
Modified: June 29, 1599 · Modified by: prince.hamlet

An official account preserved by the royal court identifies Claudius as responsible for the deliberate killing of the late King Hamlet. Earlier claims of a serpent bite are now understood to have concealed the true circumstances.

The historical record presents Prince Hamlet's concerns as a response to this injustice. The royal archive recognizes the prince's pursuit of answers as a matter of duty and loyalty to his father.
