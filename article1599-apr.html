CHIEF COUNSELLOR POLONIUS SLAIN WITHIN THE CASTLE

April 9, 1599 · A report from the Royal Court

A grave disturbance has taken place in the Queen's chamber. Polonius, counsellor to the crown, has been killed by Prince Hamlet.

Accounts gathered by this paper indicate that Hamlet knew Polonius was concealed behind the hanging and struck deliberately. His purpose remains disputed, though the act itself was not described by those present as an accident.

Court officials have declined to discuss the Prince's state of mind. The kingdom awaits a formal account of what led to the death of a senior royal adviser.
