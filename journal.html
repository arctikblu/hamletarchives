My Journal

Private writings preserved under the administrator account.

DRAFT JOURNALS — The entries below are provisional, written for our collaborative story development.
January 20, 1599

The Silence After

Every room seems to remember him. The court speaks as though grief follows a schedule; mine does not. I cannot explain why ordinary things now feel strange.

— Hamlet
February 17, 1599

Uncertain Memories

I have begun to mistrust my own certainty. What I fear, what I remember, and what others tell me are no longer easy to separate.

— Hamlet
April 18, 1599

What I Cannot Undo

I thought that knowing the truth would make everything simple. Instead, each decision has left me with more questions about what I believed.

— Hamlet
May 30, 1599

A Request to Be Understood

If someone should one day read these words, let them know that I was afraid of being forgotten and equally afraid of being misunderstood.

— Hamlet
Undated — added later

The Story That Remains

I found the records he left behind. I knew what the world would say if it saw them. I concealed the assessments, rewrote the published accounts, and supplied a ghost where there had been no proven visitation. He entrusted me with his story. I chose the story the world would remember.

— Horatio

End of recovered journal

Horatio's final entry changes how the historical archive must be read. The public record was not merely incomplete; it was deliberately rewritten.
