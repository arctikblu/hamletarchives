My Journal

Private entries saved under prince.hamlet

January 15, 1599 - Hamlet

Hamlet

January 15, 1599

Father has passed, yet every chamber seems to hold his absence. They tell me the world must go on. I do not know how they have found the strength to say it so easily.

February 12, 1599 - Hamlet

Hamlet

February 12, 1599

I distrust my own thoughts more each day. The court speaks in certainties while I wake to questions. I have mistaken fear for evidence before, and still I cannot quiet it.

My father has scarcely been laid to rest, yet my mother has already married another. Not merely another man, but his own brother. Claudius. How dare she? Has she forgotten my father so easily? Was her love for my father all but a lie?

March 19, 1599 - Hamlet

Hamlet

March 19, 1599

Claudius. That wretched man. He sits upon my father's throne, wears his crown, and dares to call himself King. I shall never forgive him.

He departed before the play had ended. I wanted his departure to mean something. Yet wanting a sign and having proof are not the same thing.

April 12, 1599 - Hamlet

Hamlet

April 12, 1599

Polonius is dead, and my hand brought about his death. I have falsely mistaken him for my devious uncle when my mother is confronting me about my hideous behaviours.

But why was he hiding? Why conceal himself behind the curtain if he had nothing to fear? Was he spying on me? Was he sent by Claudius? Yes, that must be. They are all plotting against me. Yet I cannot silence the thought that I have taken an innocent man's life. No. He was not innocent. I did what had to happen, did I not?

June 29, 1599 - Horatio

Horatio

June 29, 1599

Prince Hamlet entrusted me with his story. I found the old reports and the assessments that would define him only by his suffering and his worst decisions. I could not bear that this would be all the world remembered.

I entered his account. I removed the two assessments, changed the accounts of his father's death, the play, and Polonius, and added a occurance of the Ghost that no record could substantiate. Every public article now bears his name, though the revisions were mine.

I told myself I was saving my friend, a prince's reputation. In truth, I chose the story I wanted the world to believe.

The truth has finally come to light...

The official archive was distorted by the person trusted to preserve Hamlet's memory.
