January 15, 1599 — Hamlet — The Empty Place

Hamlet — The Empty Place

January 15, 1599

Father is gone, yet every chamber seems to hold his absence. They tell me the world must go on. I do not know how they have found the strength to say it so easily.

February 12, 1599 — Hamlet — Doubt

Hamlet — Doubt

February 12, 1599

I distrust my own thoughts more each day. The court speaks in certainties while I wake to questions. I have mistaken fear for evidence before, and still I cannot quiet it.

March 19, 1599 — Hamlet — The Performance

Hamlet — The Performance

March 19, 1599

Claudius departed before the play had ended. I wanted his departure to mean something. Yet wanting a sign and having proof are not the same thing.

April 12, 1599 — Hamlet — What Cannot Be Undone

Hamlet — What Cannot Be Undone

April 12, 1599

Polonius is dead, and my hand brought about his death. I cannot place the weight of that choice upon another. Every account I hear already seems to be turning into a story unlike the one I lived.

After the final events — Horatio — The Account That Remains

Horatio — The Account That Remains

After the final events

Hamlet entrusted me with his story. I found the old reports and the assessments that would define him only by his suffering and his worst decisions. I could not bear that this would be all the world remembered.

I entered his account. I removed the two assessments, changed the accounts of his father's death, the play, and Polonius, and added a report of the Ghost that no record could substantiate. Every public article now bears his name, though the revisions were mine.

I told myself I was saving my friend. In truth, I chose the story I wanted the world to believe. He asked me to tell his story. He never asked me to tell the truth.

The story is not the record.

The official archive was rewritten by the person trusted to preserve Hamlet's memory.
