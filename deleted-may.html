Follow-up Assessment of Prince Hamlet

May 21, 1599 · Restricted copy

Recent observations describe increasing agitation, withdrawn behaviour, and difficulty separating reported fears from independently verified events. These concerns are recorded without treating them as proof of wrongdoing.

The court is advised to distinguish between the Prince's statements, witness testimony, and confirmed events. This assessment cannot determine the truth of allegations made against other members of the royal household.
